This folder contains local browser assets for free OCR processing.

- `worker.min.js` is from Tesseract.js 7.0.0, licensed under Apache-2.0.
- `tesseract-core-simd-lstm.wasm.js` is from Tesseract.js Core 7.0.0, licensed under Apache-2.0.
- `lang/eng.traineddata.gz` and `lang/hin.traineddata.gz` are fast English and Hindi OCR language data from the Tesseract.js language-data packages. Tesseract OCR and its language data are distributed under Apache-2.0.

Upstream projects: https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js and https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast
